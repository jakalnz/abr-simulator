Labels
Wave (I-V) and category (CR/NR/INC) buttons are on the toolbar. Arm a wave (or press 1-5), then click the selected curve; with the pointer over a curve, 1-5 place the label directly (it snaps to the nearest peak). Click the armed wave again or press Esc to disarm. CR = clear response, NR = no response, INC = inconclusive (6-8 on the selected curve; press again to remove).
Move a label
Drag a label with the mouse, or click it to select it and use the buttons or the ← → keys (Shift = bigger steps). Delete removes the selected label; right-click a label for remove options.
